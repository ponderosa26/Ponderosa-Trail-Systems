Three ways to build your electrical system

Pick the package that fits your trip. Each one is a complete electrical rework for an existing overland build. Not sure which fits? Start with a consulting session.

Foundation

The strong foundational base for an existing overland build, ready to grow with you.

  • Battery-to-battery DC-DC charger (BCDC) charging your auxiliary battery from the alternator
  • Dual battery system with the starter and auxiliary batteries isolated and protected
  • Battery management and monitoring, with a live state-of-charge readout
  • Fused distribution panel with open, labeled circuits ready for accessories
  • Correctly sized cable, breakers and terminations, labeled and documented
Request a quote

Trail

Everything in Foundation, plus the power and charging to live off-grid for days.

  • Everything in Foundation
  • Roof-mounted solar with an MPPT controller feeding the auxiliary battery
  • Larger lithium auxiliary battery bank sized to your actual loads
  • Dedicated 12V fridge circuit plus switched circuits for lighting, USB and accessories
  • Inverter for 120V AC when you need it
Request a quote

Expedition

A complete electrical rework, built and proven for a worldwide overland trip.

  • Everything in Trail, sized up for extended remote travel
  • Redundant charging from the alternator (BCDC), solar and shore power, so one failure never strands you
  • Remote battery and system monitoring with fault alerts
  • Full harness rebuild with protected, serviceable routing, plus a spare fuse and parts kit
  • Load-tested and documented before you leave, with wiring diagram and owner's guide
